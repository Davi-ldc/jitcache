Requirement. SPEC-image.md section 16.1, T1: "`JSTests/jitcache/image/` holds programs that, together, make the producer emit every target kind and form of table 3.3 and every census site". Table 3.3 (section 3.3) gives `CommonThunk` the forms `Call` and `Jump`, and SPEC-image.sites.md lists rows whose target is "none".

Why the code cannot meet it as written. No baseline image or MathIC snippet reaches a common thunk through a `Call`. The census (SPEC-image.sites.md) names three `CommonThunk` sites, and each is a jump: B3, `jumpSupport` to `ThrowStackOverflowAtPrologue` in `JIT::compileAndLinkWithoutFinalizing`; A17, `linkJumpToSupport` to `HandleException` in `JIT::exceptionCheck(Jump)`; and E1, `jumpSupport` to `ReturnFromBaseline` in `JIT::emit_op_ret`. These are the only uses of `JITCacheSupportKey::commonThunk` in `jit/`, so no program can make the producer emit a `CommonThunk` `Call`. Every other pair of kind and form in table 3.3 has a census site that emits it. The rows without a target cannot reach a captured image either: A7a, A11a, A15a, A18 and D8 lie on paths no baseline compilation takes, C23 and C25 exist only in Debugger-mode bytecode (rule C3), and C20, C24 and the guard A13 make the record `Unrecordable`, so a body that meets one is never captured and a consumer has no image to check.

What the code does instead. The corpus emits every census site that has a target, which covers every pair of kind and form in table 3.3 except a `CommonThunk` `Call`. Each script names the targets it covers in its header and checks them in its twins Consumer runs through `expectTargets` in `JSTests/jitcache/image/resources/image-sections.js`, so a claim that no image backs fails the script. `CommonThunk` is claimed with `Jump` only, by exceptions.js (A17), stack-overflow.js (B3) and calls.js (E1). V3 still accepts a `CommonThunk` `Call` fixup and the writers still patch one, as table 3.3 allows. The rows without a target run under a recorder in `ImageRecordingTests.cpp` instead, which checks the reason each leaves: C20 and C24 in `imageUnrecordableBodiesAreNeverCaptured` (T11), A7a, A11a, A13, A15a and A18 in `imageSharedHelpersMarkUnannotatedReferences`, and section 4.4's guards in `imageGuardsMarkUnannotatedLinks`. D8 has no baseline caller to drive, and C23 and C25 need Debugger-mode bytecode, which no recording compilation meets (section 4.1). Nothing is asked of other tasks. The SPEC only has to say that T1 covers the pairs of kind and form that the census emits and the census sites that have a target.

Evidence. `JIT::compileAndLinkWithoutFinalizing` and `JIT::exceptionCheck(Jump)` in `jit/JIT.cpp`, and `JIT::emit_op_ret` in `jit/JITCall.cpp`, the three callers that pass `JITCacheSupportKey::commonThunk`; census rows A17, B3 and E1, and the rows whose target is "none" (A7a, A11a, A13, A15a, A18, C20, C23 to C25, D8); `expectTargets` and the claims of `exceptions.js`, `stack-overflow.js` and `calls.js` in `JSTests/jitcache/image/`; `imageUnrecordableBodiesAreNeverCaptured`, `imageSharedHelpersMarkUnannotatedReferences` and `imageGuardsMarkUnannotatedLinks` in `Source/JavaScriptCore/jitcache/tests/ImageRecordingTests.cpp`.
